# TradeWave Beta Landing Page / Product Page Guide

## Positioning
TradeWave is a creator marketplace and platform for talent, projects, and digital engagement. The product blends marketplace interactions, creator profiles, project hiring, payments, and AI-enabled support tools.

## Recommended landing page structure
### Hero section
Headline: "TradeWave — where creators, buyers, and teams connect."
Subheadline: "The beta platform for marketplace workflows, project hiring, and creator growth."
CTA buttons:
- Explore beta
- View docs
- Contact for commercial use

### Supporting points
- Creator-first marketplace
- Secure workflows for hiring and payments
- Built for community, collaboration, and project discovery
- Beta-ready product concept with active iteration

### Showcase section
Use the existing showcase mockup and add:
- Landing page screenshot
- Dashboard screenshot
- Project / creator profile mockup
- Short motion or hover interaction preview

### Social proof / product story
Use a short narrative section:
- Built around modern marketplace workflows
- Designed for creator profiles and project-based work
- Geared toward AI-enhanced analytics and productivity

### Feature list
Use 4–6 concise features:
- Creator profiles and portfolio pages
- Marketplace and hiring workflows
- Payment-ready architecture
- AI-assisted insights and dashboards
- Community discovery and engagement
- Beta-focused product iteration

### CTA footer
- "Request access"
- "View repository"
- "Contact the owner"
