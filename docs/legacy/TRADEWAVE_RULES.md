# TradeWave Platform Rules

## User Conduct
1. All users must be at least 13 years old
2. No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination
3. No spamming or bot activity
4. No impersonation of other users or entities
5. No sharing of illegal or harmful content

## Content Guidelines
1. All uploaded content must be original or properly licensed
2. No copyrighted material without permission
3. No explicit or adult content
4. No content promoting violence or illegal activities
5. No misleading or deceptive content

## Token System Rules
1. Minimum token balance: 10% of total earned tokens
2. Token rewards are non-transferable
3. Tokens have to be purchased with real money
4. Token rewards are subject to review
5. Tokens expire after 12 months of inactivity

## Hiring System Rules
1. All project terms must be clearly defined
2. Payment must be held in escrow until completion
3. Disputes must be resolved within 14 days
4. No solicitation outside the platform
5. Commission fees are non-refundable

## Content Licensing
1. Open-source projects must include proper licensing
2. Commercial use requires explicit permission
3. Attribution must be given where required
4. License terms cannot be changed after upload
5. Platform has right to remove improperly licensed content

## Payment Rules
1. All payments must go through platform
2. No direct payment between users
3. Withdrawals require minimum balance
4. Processing fees are non-refundable
5. Payment disputes must be reported within 7 days

## Streaming Rules
1. No copyrighted music without permission
2. No explicit or adult content
3. No harassment or hate speech
4. No illegal activities
5. Streams must follow community guidelines

## Account Management
1. One account per person
2. Account sharing is prohibited
3. Inactive accounts may be deactivated after 6 months
4. Account deletion requires 30 days notice
5. Platform reserves right to terminate accounts

## Privacy Rules
1. No sharing of personal information
2. No unauthorized data collection
3. No scraping or automated data gathering
4. Privacy settings must be respected
5. Data protection laws must be followed

## Platform Rights
1. Right to modify rules with notice
2. Right to remove content
3. Right to terminate accounts
4. Right to modify features
5. Right to update terms of service

## Dispute Resolution
1. Platform mediates all disputes
2. Decisions are final
3. No chargebacks allowed
4. Evidence must be provided
5. Resolution within 14 days

## Security Rules
1. Strong passwords required
2. Two-factor authentication recommended
3. No sharing of login credentials
4. Report security issues immediately
5. Follow security best practices

## Community Guidelines
1. Be respectful to all users
2. Help maintain positive environment
3. Report violations
4. No trolling or harassment
5. Support other creators

## Content Moderation
1. AI-assisted moderation
2. User reporting system
3. Manual review available
4. Appeal process exists
5. Transparent moderation policies
