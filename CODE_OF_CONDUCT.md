# Code of Conduct

This project is governed by a simple respect-first standard.

We expect all contributors and participants to:
- Be respectful, professional, and constructive.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and not on personal attacks.
- Protect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive information or secrets.
- Act in good faith and with the best interests of the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, abuse, doxxing, spam, or disruptive behavior.

If you experience or witness a problem, please contact the maintainer at:
- steurbautwaya703@gmail.com

The maintainer may take reasonable action, including warning, blocking, or removing access, when needed.
